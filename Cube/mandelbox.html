The camera never travels. Each interior is rendered into six environment-map faces once, then reused for inexpensive perspective rotation. A brief preparation pass happens on first use or when detail changes. Afterward, the renderer sleeps until you interact.

Folded core uses a finite Mandelbox estimator. The other two are deliberately simpler, cube-based interpretations. Nothing is downloaded from the network.
