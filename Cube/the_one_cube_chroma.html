Only the two original-base paths remain, each adapted around the uploaded cube’s 24 unique vertices. Folded core feeds the bevel planes into its recurrence; Cathedral repeats the seed in four scales of solids and apertures. These are artistic adaptations, not unchanged mathematical originals.

Lighting and material weights are cached in a cubemap. Your palette is applied afterward, so shuffling does not rerun the geometry shader. Tints follow faces, bevels, and recursive detail—not the screen. Both paths share the same active colors. Nothing moves automatically, and the renderer sleeps when idle.

The six defaults are in PALETTE, near the top of app.js (also inline in this HTML). No network, fonts, textures, or model files are loaded.
