FIELD NOTES / GENERATION & BUILD

Make a little world.
Give it room to grow.

Build cameraDrag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag to pan. Home frames the world. Alt-drag also orbits while placing or removing. World layoutEvery level is 4 × 4 × 4 chunks: 64 chunks, each 64³ blocks, making a fixed 256³ world. There is no layout selector. The atlas still contains 27 levels. Place / removePlace a single 1³ control cube: one platform grows directly underneath it, with the cube centered on top. Its footprint and half-block growth are owned by that cube. Delete the cube to remove the entire owned platform and its local branch. Existing generated terrain and other platforms are preserved. Choose a palette colour before placing; in Select, swatches recolour the cube, its platform, growth, and branch. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 choose Select / Place / Remove. Platform sizeKeep the original 16–24 seeded roll: 16–21 stay unchanged; 22 becomes 44, 23 becomes 46, and 24 becomes 48 blocks across. Only the footprint doubles; the four-layer platform thickness stays unchanged. No size slider. Manual platforms pin their size, growth, and detail seed on placement, so generating or shuffling the main world keeps them stable. An even-sized owned platform uses a half-block finishing rim so its 1³ control cube is exactly centered. Small-cube growthEach platform independently rolls 250–500% of the original full-strength growth pass. The seeded roll forms connected half-block shelves and stepped skirts. No growth slider. The checkbox disables growth without losing the individual rolls. Local branchesWith automatic paths on, each owned platform gets one link to existing generated terrain. In a blank world, the first cube grows an isolated platform; later platforms connect to earlier ones. With automatic paths off, the platforms remain and only the paths are hidden. Removing an empty-world anchor repairs only dependent links. World 27 offers all six colours plus Void. Undo / redoCtrl or Cmd + Z / Shift + Z. History is independent for every world. Ctrl or Cmd + Y also redoes. Portal labHalo, Terraced, and Twin ring are live frame designs. Adjust opening and depth; rotate the colour seams. The portal’s destination determines one solid colour, equal halves, or equal thirds. Void-bound portals have seven rainbow bands; Leave stays neutral. Whole-cube rounding differs by at most one cube. Flat colours disables portal lighting for a palette check. Portal inspectionChoose a face, then Front or Angle to inspect that actual portal using the build camera. World or Home frames the whole scene. The optional 2 × 2 guide contains display-only 1³ blocks and does not add terrain or count toward the budget. Portal pathsWith generated platforms and Automatic paths enabled, straight 2 × 2 block connections run through each portal centre. The main ribbons stay three wide and two thick. A fixed maximum frame envelope keeps all design settings collision-free without rebuilding terrain. Face portals stay inside the boundary; Void also keeps its centered Leave portal. Destination labels select a world for editing, not gameplay. Save / importExport saves all 27 levels as an authoring JSON project. Older projects expand to 4³, keeping authored world-space positions. Their old size and growth sliders are replaced with individual rolls. Portal circles and centered sockets replace the old frames, so generated paths can change. Pre-v9 control cubes gain owned platforms, and their local paths move down to meet the platform tops. New v9 recipes retain their owned-platform rolls. The 22–24 generated rolls double to 44–48, so derived terrain changes. An authored object inside a new portal clearance or outside the world is rejected rather than silently moved or deleted. No player or game-camera settings are exported.

All 27 worlds start completely empty. Generation is explicit. Control cubes and their owned platforms survive regeneration. Remove a control cube to remove its platform, growth, and branch; the generated main network is separate. 16 is a soft budget, not a hard cap. JSON includes generation recipes, per-world portal designs, cube colours, pinned owned-platform rolls, and saved local branch centerlines—not a game-specific loader.
